About the Studio

VRG DESIGN DEN

VRG DESIGN DEN is an interdisciplinary, excellence and innovation-based company. Founded on the belief that architecture has a powerful impact on enriching people’s life, as well as having massive influences in enhancing and improving the world we live in. Based in Chennai, we specialize in architecture, landscape, interior design and custom furnishings. Our works range from luxury multi-family residential to bespoke commercial, landscaping and hospitality interiors. We strive to innovate and create unique end-user experiences that inspire and maximize worthwhile aesthetic.

What We Do

Our Services

Our design team customizes a tailored plan to suit all lifestyles, demographic regions, and budgets to resonate with each client's target market. We value function, comfort, and beauty which we achieve through classic design practices and an artistic approach to composition.

01

ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN

We believe our essence in design is creating well-designed buildings to enrich peoples experience and improve their quality of life. Along with our clients, we create buildings that are healthy, comfortable, flexible, simple and efficient. They span into two different categories: Public Buildings (hospitals, malls, civic centres, educational buildings recreational buildings), and Residential Buildings. (Apartments, villas, hotels, motels, etc.)

02

PLANNING & URBAN DESIGN

In VRG Design Den, we design in a full Integration with nature shaping sustainable communities friendly environments innovative and creative urban and rural communities aiming to improve the quality of life of today and future generations. Planning in VRG DESIGN DEN involves in a wide range of services: Large scale planning projects (new cities districts neighbourhoods), Small scale projects (resorts and compounds), Urban Design (Public parks and landscape, social and sports club, etc.), In addition to conservation and renovation to historical sites and buildings.

03

LANDSCAPE DESIGN

We create innovative and aesthetically pleasing environments for people to enjoy. We design landscape on two different levels urban level and details level believing that every single detail enriches and shapes our landscape. We design with advanced standards for different projects starting from city planning to residential compounds and villa, as well as public buildings and recreational areas including the design for plants hardscape, lighting, Irrigation and street furnishing.

04

INTERIOR DESIGN

Interior design enriches the life of its users in luxurious way believing in the visual Impacts of interior and echo of material on their productivity, greater creativity and innovation while sustaining a comfortable and flexible use. We design in different scale from product design to huge public and residential buildings. This includes but not limited to finishing material, lighting units distribution and choice of furnishing elements.

05

TURNKEY CONSTRUCTION

Turnkey projects are considered to be the trend of the decade as it saves time, effort and money for clients in many ways possible. Our Turnkey projects involves planning, designing and executing both interiors and exteriors of the projects as per the requirements of the client and hand over the key to them after the final phase. All they need to do is to share their ideas, budget and requirements. In our works, we always focus on delivering quality projects that ensure sustainability and innovation

06

ANIMATION & VISUALIZATION

We specialize in several photo-realistic visualizations. Our work is characterized by the highest realistic qualities. We offer our clients the ability to create photorealistic simulations that best-demonstrate the product, providing them with a virtual-liveable experience of the designed spaces from different views and angles. We give our clients more than just production services as we offer our design and creativity solutions throughout the entire production process. Excellence and Innovation are the core values in our work that we always deliver.

Our Workflow

The Design Process

01

Discovery & Brief

We collaborate closely to explore your lifestyle requirements, aesthetic tastes, site parameters, and project goals to build a detailed design brief.

02

Concept & Planning

Drafting space allocations, technical zoning schemas, architectural floorplans, and flow dynamics tailored specifically to enrich your daily experiences.

03

3D Visualization

Creating photo-realistic visual simulations that demonstrate materials, lighting profiles, and interior accents, offering a virtual-liveable preview.

04

Detailed Design Development

Every approved concept is transformed into a complete set of technical drawings and specifications, ensuring every material, junction, dimension, and detail is precisely defined before execution begins.

05

Turnkey Construction

Direct execution, vendor supervision, technical detailing, and premium material curation, delivering the keys to your completed dream space.
